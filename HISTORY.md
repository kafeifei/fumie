# Project history

The initial Fumie history is curated into capability milestones. These commits
record the public integration work; their dates are not a reconstruction of
when each experiment originally began.

1. **Research project and distribution identity** — the project purpose,
   Fumie application identity, independent data namespace, and documentation.
2. **Build and SDK tooling** — source development, SDK pins and acquisition,
   extension packaging, and local development builds.
3. **Agent and session workbench** — harness integration, model configuration,
   persistent sessions, workspaces, permissions, and the shared chat interface.
4. **Chinese localization** — the bundled Simplified Chinese language pack.
5. **Browser and mobile entry** — optional remote-host research interfaces.

## Upstream

Fumie is based on [Code - OSS](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode). Its history
retains the original upstream commits and their authors, including the
[main-line foundation](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/7c54fda801c73e35072ac759b6d93f8c69c65d7b)
and the
[agent-workbench development branch](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/df53daabb18cd157bdb08c7f01c34df936cf12f4)
integrated into the workbench milestone. Curated Fumie commits describe
integration and adaptation; they do not claim exclusive authorship of
upstream code. Original copyright headers, `LICENSE.txt`, and
`ThirdPartyNotices.txt` remain in place.
